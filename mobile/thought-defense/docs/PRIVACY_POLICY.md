## What stays on your device

- Welcome dismiss, soft-goal progress, tip chips, and stub purchase entitlements use on-device storage (AsyncStorage)
- No Thought Defense account or cloud save in the current vertical-slice build

## What we do not collect

- No analytics SDK shipped by default
- No contact list, location, or health data
- No therapy notes or diagnosis information

## Purchases

- Paid items (Clarity Pass, looks, optional Clarity boost) are processed by Apple App Store and Google Play
- Billing and receipts follow those stores’ privacy policies
- Restore purchases anytime from Settings
- Development stub purchases store entitlements only on your device

## If we add more later

Crash reporting, accounts, or optional analytics — if ever added — will be disclosed here with opt-in where required before they ship.

## Not medical advice

Thought Defense uses a soft mental metaphor for entertainment. It does not provide therapy, diagnosis, treatment, or crisis support. If you are in distress, seek real-world help.
