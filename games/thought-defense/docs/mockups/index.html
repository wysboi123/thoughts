Upgrade UI drafts — choose / tweak

Three draft templates for Thought Defense’s explicit Upgrade button. Soft pastel · pad-based TD · ToS-safe. Specs also in ../UI_MOCKUPS.md. Nothing ships in Luau until you pick (or hybrid + tweaks).

How to reply: say A, B, or C, then any tweaks (e.g. “B, but Upgrade on the left; keep pad-click as shortcut”). Or edit DESIGN_CONSULT.md / email.
DRAFT A

Selection panel

Tap a planted thought → side/bottom panel with stats + Upgrade + Sell.

  • Best for teaching what each thought does
  • Prevents accidental upgrades
  • Heavier chrome on small phones (use bottom sheet)
